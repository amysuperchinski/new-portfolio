## Overview

**Vision Next** is Fiserv’s next-generation card issuing and payment processing platform — built from scratch, cloud-native and API-first. It replaces older batch-processing systems with real-time, event-driven processing, and manages credit, debit, prepaid, commercial cards, buy-now-pay-later and loans on a single platform.

Through Globant, I design the end-to-end experience of the **Customer Service Management (CSM)** application: the tool support agents use to handle live customer interactions, as the platform scales from internal build to its first global clients.
